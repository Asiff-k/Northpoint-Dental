## What to avoid (seen on these sites)

- Dated look: black header with red and purple buttons, all-caps serif headlines (Smile Cliniq)
- Keyword-stuffed headings ("Chelsea dentists" in every card title)
- External booking portals that leave the site: we keep booking in-page
- Award walls taking a full screen: one compact strip is enough

## Visual direction for Northpoint

Keep our calm deep teal + mint system, but borrow Dr Rez's premium feel: generous dark section for "Why choose us", thin line icons, warm cream (`warm`) review cards, slow fades. Aim for "boutique clinic", not "NHS surgery" and not "spa".
